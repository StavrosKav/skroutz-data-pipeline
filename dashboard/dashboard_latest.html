How it works

A scheduled pipeline scrapes Skroutz listings for phones, laptops, smartwatches and tablets once a day, cleans them and stores one price snapshot per product per day in PostgreSQL. This page is rebuilt from that database after every run.

  • Price drops compare each product with its previous snapshot. Drops of 40% or more are marked suspect (usually a variant mix-up) and left out of the counts.
  • Near all-time low uses the lowest price ever recorded for the product.
  • Stability is the 30-day coefficient of variation of the price; the sparkline shows the last 30 days.
  • The badge at the top shows how old the data is and turns red if a daily run was missed.
