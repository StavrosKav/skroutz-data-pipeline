How it works

A scheduled pipeline scrapes Skroutz listings for phones, laptops, smartwatches and tablets once a day, cleans them and stores one price snapshot per product per day in PostgreSQL. This page is rebuilt from that database after every run.

  • Price drops compare today's price with the lower of yesterday's price and the median of the previous 7 snapshots (at least 3 needed), so a one-day spike that returns to normal is not a drop. Drops of 40% or more are counted and marked big drop, check.
  • Near all-time low (Products filter) uses the lowest price ever recorded for the product.
  • Buy or Wait compares today's price with the product's own low, median and high over the last 90 days, using only the days we recorded (at least 20). It ignores products not listed in the latest scrape.
  • Category Price Index (Overview) follows a fixed basket: the products of each category listed on the first recorded day of the window and on at least 80% of its recorded days. Each day it shows the geometric mean of their price relative to that first day (first day = 100; 102 means about 2% dearer), so new or vanished products do not move it. Typical prices on the cards and trend charts are medians.
  • All prices are the cheapest shop's “from” price on Skroutz, not one fixed shop.
  • Stability is the 30-day coefficient of variation of the price; the sparkline shows the last 30 days.
  • The badge at the top shows how old the data is and turns red if a daily run was missed.
